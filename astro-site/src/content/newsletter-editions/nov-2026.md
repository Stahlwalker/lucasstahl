A little off topic, but I'm a bit of an Apple fanboy and just upgraded to the iPhone Duo from an iPhone 14 Pro Max. Here are my thoughts.

Face ID was the thing I thought I'd miss most, and I don't. With Touch ID and my Apple Watch it unlocks fast. The annoyances are mostly software. My fantasy football app doesn't fill the full width of the screen yet, and when the phone is closed the side navigation takes up a lot of space. Both should get fixed over time, but responsiveness is on the app developers. Hardware quirks include volume buttons that sit on top when it's closed, and a case that doesn't cover the hinge feels awkward. After going back and holding a Pro Max, I think the Duo is a good size.

Not perfect, but I dig it.
