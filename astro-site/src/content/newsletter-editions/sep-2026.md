Agents, agents, agents. This month's issue is mostly agents doing things without being watched: agents importing Figma designs, agents writing to git without a human checking the diff, agents running for months without a check-in. Somewhere in there, TypeSafe shipped a model that can't hallucinate because it never generates strings in the first place. Make of that what you will.

Also inside: a macOS notch that tracks whether your six different coding assistants are working or stuck, and a dictionary for the fifty new terms you now have to know to talk about any of this. Dig in below.
