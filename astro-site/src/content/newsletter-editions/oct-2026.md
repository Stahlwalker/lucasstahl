Welcome back. I've been heads down on product launches around Webflow's MCP server, with a lot of new features shipping back to back. Working that close to the details got me thinking about AI as a whole. Between the new terms, the big claims, and the weekly announcements, it's hard to tell what's worth your time. So this issue is a mix of things I found useful and a few I'm still chewing on.

## Links I like

- **The Story of VS Code**: A documentary on VS Code's ten-year path, from a small Zurich team building browser tools under the name Monaco, to an Electron desktop editor, open source, and the shift to AI. Told by the engineers who built it. [Read more](https://youtu.be/kHL3XzjpT5w?si=mqd9kVYDsFRALQBQ)
- **Muse Connector Platform**: Meta's directory for connectors that plug third-party products into Muse, its personal AI agent. Describe your product, submit it for functional, security, and legal review, and it appears in Muse's directory once approved. [Read more](https://muse.ai/platform)
- **omp**: A terminal coding agent with LSP and DAP built in, plus subagents, plan mode, and memory across sessions. It runs on a native Rust core and works on Windows without WSL. [Read more](https://omp.sh/)
- **How we made claude.ai 3x faster in two weeks**: Anthropic ran a performance sprint from one Slack channel with Claude in every thread and merged 3,000+ changes with no rollbacks. The takeaway is that once Claude has a number to beat, it can climb it, so the team kept finding more things to measure. [Read more](https://claude.dev/blog/how-we-made-claude-ai-faster/)
  _Raymond Wang, Sam Attard & Issac G._
